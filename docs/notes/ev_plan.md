# Project Plan

Look at the how the structure of the networks through time contribute to the “ecological feasibility” of the plants and pollinators working within them (i.e., the ability to maintain positive abundances with perturbation).

## Research plan

### Exploratory analysis

- repeat the analysis at both the individual site level (repeat for each site) and with all of the sites included (combine all sites)
- build networks for each week
- calculate the feasibility domain (cone)

### Incorporating environmental data

- Extract environmental data from the RMBL
  - at the annual level
    - snowfall
    - snowmelt date
  - at the site level
    - elevation
    - surrounding habitat
    - potantial other facotrs of interest
  - at the week level
    - mean temp
    - max temp
    - min temp
    - temp range
    - precipitation
    - other important contributions to pollinator activity
- Community context data
  - total pollinators
  - diversity and abundance of floral resources
    - presumaby these are different throughout the season and at each site

## Questions and hypotheses

- How does the feasibility domain change within and across domains?
  - H1: the feasibility domain increases and then decreases throughout the season, forming a hump shape. (greatest in the mid-season with the greatest diversity)
  - H2: the mean feasibility domain is relatively consistent between years
- How do environmental characteristics shape the feasibility domain?
  - harsh conditions reduce feasibility.
